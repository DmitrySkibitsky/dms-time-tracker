### Added

- Clockify provider behind a provider-neutral `TrackerProvider` interface.
- Bar widget, popout (timer, history, entry editor, manual time), Control Center tile.
- Settings: service credentials, workspace, default project and task, sync, history, bar options.
- IPC: `toggle`, `start`, `stop`, `continueLast`, `refresh`, `status`.
- English, Russian and Ukrainian translations.
